    Questions, answered.

    Anything else? Email us.

    How quickly can someone start?

    We propose candidates within 48 hours of a scoping call. Most people are working in your environment within days of you choosing.

    Who manages the consultant day to day?

    With staff augmentation and dedicated resources, you do: they work in your tools and ceremonies. With a fixed-scope project or delivery team, we own delivery and report to you.

    Which parts of Dynamics 365 do you cover?

    Finance & Operations, Business Central, Customer Engagement, Sales and Field Service, Supply Chain and WMS, and the Power Platform, plus Azure integration, Dataverse and data migration.

    How do you vet people?

    On real project experience, not certificates alone. Every candidate has delivered on the module and version you need.

    Can we swap or scale the team later?

    Yes. Add capacity, swap a skill set or wind down on notice, without renegotiating the whole engagement.

    Do you support systems after go-live?

    Yes. Managed support runs on tiered SLAs with round-the-clock coverage for live environments.
